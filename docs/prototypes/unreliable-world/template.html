What this prototype models

A 48-second repeating scenario. Available links require distance within the chosen range, no intersection with a building or mountain obstacle volume, and both endpoints online. Ground 3 is offline from 16 to 25 seconds when outages are enabled. Terrain coordinates and elevations use arbitrary world units.

Lines show instantaneous link availability, not delivered messages. This standalone model is not connected to the SwarmSync research engine. It does not model radio propagation, packet queues, latency or acknowledgements. Ground height is an arbitrary schematic radio elevation above flat ground; raised nodes can be suspended and do not automatically attach to terrain; scenery is simplified. Only the main building and ridge volumes block links; trees, crates and roadside props are decorative. Region counts follow moving devices across boundaries. Increasing aircraft height can clear obstacles but also increase distance.
